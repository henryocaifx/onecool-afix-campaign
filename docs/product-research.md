## Positioning and audience

The public application presents a filmmaking suite organized around characters, scripts, and cinematic video. Its named workspaces include Cast, CasTequila, Cine, CinePie, MotionMate, Movi Board, Movi, Movi2 (SD2.5), and Movi3. The likely audience is creative filmmakers, visual storytellers, and small production teams; this is an inference from the tools and copy rather than verified user research.

The public home page describes Cast as a digital-character portrait workspace, CasTequila as a character-sheet tool, Cine as a script-to-storyboard tool, and Movi Board as a storyboard-animation workspace. These descriptions verify the application's published positioning, not generation quality, execution time, reliability, or successful end-to-end production.

## Campaign constraints

- Campaign line: **Make room for your imagination.** This is an invitation, not a performance or feature guarantee.
- Closing product descriptor: **AI-powered filmmaking**, supported by the live application's positioning.
- Connect each imaginative breakthrough to a real AIFX Studio workspace capture. Do not fabricate interface controls, generation success messages, or speed claims.
- No claim that a complete film is produced in one click, that deadlines are guaranteed, or that character consistency is perfect.
- Public site is English; campaign copy and sparse dialogue use English.

Authentic public interface: nearly black surfaces, white sans-serif type, violet-to-magenta-to-cyan accents, a yellow ONECOOL AIFX header mark, and compact technical labels.
