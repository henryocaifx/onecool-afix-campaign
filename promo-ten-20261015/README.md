# ONECOOL AIFX · Ten Ways Into Wonder

Ten distinct fun, immersive campaign films. Requested: 30 seconds, 1080p, 21:9, native synchronized music/effects, no watermark. Casts include people, animated objects and abstract light; no animals or titles/subtitles/text boxes. The authentic brand mark is requested on physical film equipment.

See [treatments](docs/treatments.md) and [exact API prompts](records/films.json). Each MP4 will be uploaded to private R2 unchanged from Seedance, with no transcoding or post-editing. Native codec, exact dimensions and timing are retained and documented. Videos, signed URLs and credentials are excluded from Git.
